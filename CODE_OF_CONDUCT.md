# Code of conduct

MyTV Launcher is a small project that people work on in their spare time. To keep it pleasant for everyone:

- Be kind and respectful. Disagree about the work, not about the person.
- Assume good intentions, and be patient with people who are new, who write in a second language, or who use a TV you
  have never heard of.
- Stay on the subject of the issue or pull request.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual language or imagery, or publishing someone's private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and other contributions that do not fit this, and may block people
who keep ignoring it.

To report behaviour that does not belong here, contact the maintainer privately through the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/tinteractive). Reports are treated confidentially.
